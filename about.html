Our mission

Cybersecurity knowledge should be accessible.

Cypher-School brings together beginner-friendly learning material and practical guidance for people who want to pursue cybersecurity responsibly.

Why Cypher-School exists

We are building a free resource hub for ethical hacking and cybersecurity education. The goal is to help learners understand how technology can be protected, not misused.

Every path on Cypher-School will encourage legal, permission-based practice and respect for people, systems, and privacy.
